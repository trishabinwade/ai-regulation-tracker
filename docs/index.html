Method

How the tracker works

Live legislation

Federal bills via the Congress.gov API; state bills via OpenStates — refreshed every build, newest action first.

Real litigation

Federal dockets and published opinions from CourtListener's free database. No headlines, no punditry.

Verified enactments

A law is listed as enacted only when confirmed against an official government source. Convincing but unconfirmed candidates are flagged Unverified and excluded from the count.

Mapped frameworks

19 voluntary frameworks, codes and principles — NIST, ISO, OECD and more — each cross-walked to the binding laws it overlaps with, so soft law can be read against hard law.

About this tracker

A public index of artificial-intelligence law and litigation: US federal and state bills, AI lawsuits in federal court, and enacted AI laws worldwide. Every enacted law is verified against an official government source; reported laws that cannot be confirmed are marked Unverified and kept out of the enacted count.
