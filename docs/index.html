Enacted AI Laws Worldwide

Hand-curated, verified list of AI laws actually in force. Maintained in data/global_laws.json — edit it directly to add new laws.

Enacted

Artificial Intelligence Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689

European Union · Enacted: June 13, 2024 (adopted) · In force: August 1, 2024

The world's first comprehensive AI law: risk-tiered rules (prohibited / high-risk / transparency duties), conformity assessments for high-risk systems, and obligations for general-purpose AI models. Phased application through 2027–2028.

Enacted

Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services

China · Enacted: July 13, 2023 (issued) · In force: August 15, 2023

Requires generative AI services offered to the Chinese public to align with socialist values, complete security assessments and algorithm filings before launch, and respect IP and personal-information protections. Issued jointly by the CAC and six other regulators.

Enacted

AI Basic Act (Framework Act on AI Development and Establishment of Trust)

South Korea · Enacted: December 26, 2024 (passed); promulgated January 21, 2025 · In force: January 22, 2026

Consolidated 19 prior bills into Asia's first comprehensive AI framework. Risk-based duties for 'high-impact' AI (impact assessments, human oversight), generative-output labeling, and extraterritorial reach; fines up to KRW 30 million.

Enacted

AI Promotion Act (Act No. 53 of 2025)

Japan · Enacted: May 28, 2025 (passed by the Diet) · In force: June 4, 2025 (most provisions); September 1, 2025 (fully)

Japan's first AI-specific national law. Innovation-first: no fines or penalties — the government may issue guidance, request information, or publicly name non-compliant entities. Established an AI Strategy Headquarters.

Enacted

Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA), HB 149

United States — Texas · Enacted: June 22, 2025 (signed) · In force: January 1, 2026

Comprehensive state AI statute: prohibits AI used to manipulate behavior, enable unlawful discrimination, or generate CSAM; bans government social scoring; creates a 36-month regulatory sandbox and AI advisory council. Enforced exclusively by the Texas AG.

Enacted

Connecticut AI Responsibility and Transparency (CART) Act, SB 5

United States — Connecticut · Enacted: June 2, 2026 (signed) · In force: October 1, 2026 (key provisions)

Omnibus state AI law: anti-discrimination rules for automated employment decision tools, deployer disclosure duties, AI subscription and chatbot provisions, and whistleblower protections for frontier-AI developers. Notice requirements phase in through 2028.

Enacted

TAKE IT DOWN Act (Public Law 119-12)

United States · Enacted: May 19, 2025 (signed) · In force: May 19, 2025 (criminal provisions); May 19, 2026 (platform notice-and-removal duties)

Federal law criminalizing knowing publication of authentic or AI-generated nonconsensual intimate imagery; requires covered platforms to establish a notice-and-removal process with 48-hour removal on valid request.

Enacted

AI Policy Act (SB 149)

United States — Utah · Enacted: March 13, 2024 (signed) · In force: May 1, 2024

First U.S. state law specifically regulating generative AI: requires disclosure when consumers interact with AI (on request generally, proactively in regulated occupations), establishes liability for AI-driven consumer-protection violations, and creates an Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy. Amended by SB 226 (effective May 7, 2025).

Enacted

Transparency in Frontier AI Act (SB 53)

United States — California · Enacted: September 29, 2025 (signed) · In force: January 1, 2026

Requires large frontier-AI developers to publish safety and security frameworks, report critical safety incidents to the state, and implement whistleblower protections; the industry-backed successor to the vetoed SB 1047.

Enacted

Provisions on the Administration of Algorithm Recommendation in Internet Information Services

China · Enacted: December 31, 2021 (promulgated) · In force: March 1, 2022

China's foundational algorithm regulation (joint CAC/MIIT/MPS/SAMR order): requires algorithm filing with the CAC, user disclosure and opt-out of algorithmic recommendations, non-discrimination, and protections for minors, the elderly, and gig workers; covers generation-synthesis (deepfake) algorithms alongside recommenders.
